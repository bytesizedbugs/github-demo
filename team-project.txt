Project ideas:
- Poem

"Roses are red,
violets are blue,
the sun climbs slowly
over the hills we knew.

Morning spills gold
on the sleeping town,
and every window
wears a little crown.

The river hums a tune
it learned long ago,
while willows bend to listen
and bees begin to glow.

We walked the winding path
where daisies lean and sway,
and talked of all the things
we hoped to be someday.

Clouds drift like sailing ships
across a sea of sky,
carrying our wishes
to wherever wishes fly.

Night folds the world in blankets,
stitched with silver light,
and whispers to the sleepers
that everything is right.
